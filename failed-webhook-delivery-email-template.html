Failed webhook delivery checklist

Use this before sending a customer-facing failed webhook delivery notice. Keep raw payloads, endpoint secrets, signing secrets, authorization headers, tokens, customer lists, internal logs, stack traces, and private endpoint details out of public tools.

1. State the delivery scope

  • Which endpoint, destination, app, workspace, or customer integration is affected?
  • Which event type, object/entity, and delivery window are involved?
  • What was the latest delivery outcome: status code, timeout, TLS issue, redirect, or network error?

2. Explain retry state

  • How many attempts have happened?
  • When was the latest attempt?
  • When is the next retry, or are automatic retries exhausted?
  • What customer action is needed before retry succeeds?

3. Give a recovery path

  • Can support manually resend, replay, backfill, or provide a reconciliation list?
  • Who owns the support handoff and next update?
  • What should the customer check: endpoint availability, POST handling, 2xx response, TLS, WAF, firewall, or allowlist?

4. Avoid duplicate and privacy risk

  • Add an idempotency or duplicate-processing caveat when replay, retry, resend, or backfill is discussed.
  • Do not guarantee no data loss unless that has been validated by approved systems.
  • Avoid customer blame, fake certainty, private payload excerpts, raw headers, tokens, and signing secrets.
